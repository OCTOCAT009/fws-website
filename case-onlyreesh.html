Case Study · Artist Platform · 2026

Only.Reesh.

Reesh — independent artist & Bollytekk pioneer

SectorMusic
DisciplineImmersive Web
Year2026
ScopeDesign + WebGL

The Brief

Reesh is an independent singer, composer and producer — founder of Bollytekk, a desi-fusion subgenre. The site had to feel like the music: cinematic, spiritual, and built for atmosphere before a single word is read.

For a musician, a conventional website is close to useless. Nobody reads an artist bio to decide whether they like the work. The site has to do what the first eight bars do.

What We Built

An immersive entry with a custom WebGL starfield and a gate animation that has to be passed through rather than scrolled past. A custom identity system in motion. Behind it, a clean discography that links straight out to the music rather than trying to hold you.

Full atmosphere, zero templates — and no page builder doing the heavy lifting.

Why It Matters

Some sites explain who you are. This one sets a mood, and then gets out of the way.
