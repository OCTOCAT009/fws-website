We are actually here. Founded in Delhi, building for brands across NCR — and for anyone else who finds us.

A Delhi studio, deliberately small

Forth Worth Studios was founded in Delhi by two people, and it has stayed that size on purpose. There is no account layer between you and the people building the thing — the person you brief is the person who writes the code.

That is most of why the pricing works. A traditional agency carries project managers, an office and a sales team, and that overhead lands in your quote whether or not it improves your website.

Working with us across NCR

Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad are all a normal part of how we work. In practice almost everything happens over calls and shared links regardless of distance, but if a first meeting in person is what makes you comfortable committing budget, that is easy to arrange here in a way it is not for our overseas clients.

Common questions

Can we meet in person?
In Delhi NCR, yes, if it helps. We will be straight with you though: the work itself happens over calls and shared links, and a meeting is about confidence rather than progress.
Do you only work with Delhi clients?
No. Delhi is where we are, not who we serve. Roughly half our work is elsewhere in India and we take international clients too.
How quickly can you start?
Usually within a week or two, depending on what is in the queue. A ₹2,250 audit is a good way to start immediately while a larger build is being scoped.
Tell us what you're building

The vision, the budget, the references. We reply fast and we will tell you if we are the wrong studio for it.
