Web Design For UK Brands.

A Delhi studio working with UK businesses — with a genuine overlap window every working day, and prices published instead of quoted.

Being straight about where we are

We do not have a London office. We are in Delhi, we work with UK clients remotely, and we would rather lead with that than let you find out later.

How the overlap actually works

London is four and a half hours behind Delhi in summer and five and a half in winter. Our afternoon is your morning, which gives a real shared window every working day — enough for a call and a round of decisions before we carry on into our evening while you finish your day.

In practice this means you often wake up to work that happened overnight. That is an advantage if the brief was clear, and a frustration if it was not — so we front-load the scoping.

What you get out of it

  • Delhi pricing on UK work. A custom multi-page build starts at ₹15,000 — roughly £340. A London studio quoting the same brief is in a different bracket entirely, and the difference is largely overhead.
  • No account manager. Two people, and the person you brief writes the code.
  • You own the code outright. No licence, no platform lock-in, no retainer needed to keep it alive.

The honest caveats

We invoice in GBP or USD. We are not a UK company, so if procurement requires a domestic supplier we are not the right fit — worth checking before we both spend time on it. And we are small by choice: a limited number of projects run at once.
