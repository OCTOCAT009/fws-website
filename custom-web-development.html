Custom Web Development.

Built from an empty file, for the business you actually run — not bent to fit assumptions a theme author made about someone else.

What "custom" buys you

Not beauty. A competent designer makes a template beautiful too. Custom buys three specific things, and if you do not need all three you probably should not pay for it:

  • Only the code you need. Templates ship every feature every buyer might want and you load all of it. This is why custom sites are usually faster despite doing more.
  • No ceiling. The answer to "can it do this?" is always yes; it is only ever a question of budget. With a template, the answer is eventually no.
  • Ownership. No licence, no plugin subscriptions, no platform that can change its pricing on you. You can take the code to another developer tomorrow.

Where it stops being a website

The moment something has to remember — accounts, orders, bookings, stock, submissions — you have crossed from a website into software. That is a different discipline and a different price, and being clear about which side of the line you are on is most of getting the quote right.

We build both. A returns engine with live order verification, a mail system that batches sends and survives a daily cap, a client portal with roles and permissions — those are the projects most studios quote and then quietly subcontract.

Common questions

How long does a custom website take?
A one-page Launch build is a few days. A Signature multi-page site is usually two to three weeks. Flagship work with a custom backend runs longer and is scoped in fortnightly cycles so you see it working as it goes rather than at the end.
Do I own the code?
Yes, outright, with no licence and nothing to keep paying for. It is worth asking every quote you receive this exact question — if the answer is anything other than a plain yes, you are renting a website rather than buying one.
Can you take over a site someone else built?
If it is custom-coded, usually yes. If it is a page-builder site, taking it over often costs more than rebuilding, because every change has to fight the builder. A ₹2,250 audit will tell you which it is before you commit.
