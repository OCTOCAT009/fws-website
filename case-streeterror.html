Street Error.

Mumbai streetwear — with systems no template ships with

SectorStreetwear
LocationMumbai
Year2026
ScopeStore + Backend

The Client

Street Error is a Mumbai streetwear label — limited drops, no mass production. Streets will remember. They needed a store that felt like the brand rather than a theme, and back-office systems that actually run without someone babysitting a spreadsheet.

What We Built

A custom theme from scratch: a full-screen no-scroll intro, a floating product "space" instead of a grid, and a slide-out cart that never takes you off the page you were browsing.

Then the hard part. A custom returns and exchange system with live order verification against real orders, request tracking IDs, a logging backend, and automated store-credit refunds. Most stores handle returns over WhatsApp and lose the thread; this one handles them as a system.

Plus cash on delivery done properly — with an enforced convenience fee rather than an honour-system checkbox — and full SEO and schema baked in from the start.

Why It Matters

A storefront that sells, and a back office that runs itself. The second half is the one that decides whether a small team survives a drop day.
