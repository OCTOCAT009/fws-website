Who We Are

The Studio
That Was
Always Missing.

Forth Worth Studios builds the websites brands deserve — premium-level craft, without the premium-level gatekeeping.

01

The Origin.

Forth Worth started from frustration. We kept watching brands with real soul — people pouring everything into their music, their clothing, their vision — get handed websites that looked like everyone else's. Dragged-and-dropped. Templated. Forgettable.

The good studios charged like banks. The affordable ones shipped recycled themes. There was nothing in between for the people who actually needed it most — the independent creators, the first-time founders, the ones building something that means something.

So we built the studio we always wished existed. No templates. No page builders. No recycled layouts. Every project starts from a blank file and a real conversation.

02

How We Work.

We treat design, code, motion and copy as one piece — not separate steps handed between people who never talk. That's why our sites feel whole instead of assembled.

Every project gets our full weight. Whether it's your first brand or your tenth, you get the same obsessive care — the kind that sweats the loading animation, the micro-interaction, the way the type sits at 2am.

"We don't do half-measures. If it's not hitting, we keep going."

And we build things most studios won't touch — custom returns systems, AI agents, gamified experiences, WebGL worlds. If you can imagine it, that's the starting line.

Meet The Founders

Two Minds.
One Studio.

Forth Worth Studios runs on two opposite instincts — strategy and creativity — meeting in the middle of every project.

UM
Utsav MadaanFounder & Brand Strategist

Utsav brings the strategic foundation behind Forth Worth Studios.

Currently pursuing his undergraduate studies at Shri Ram College of Commerce (SRCC), he has worked across consulting, research, and corporate environments, gaining experience through projects involving brand strategy, business analysis, consumer understanding, and growth-focused decision making.

Having worked with organizations such as ITC and contributed to multiple research and consulting initiatives, Utsav approaches every project from a business-first perspective. His focus is ensuring that every website aligns with the client's brand identity, customer expectations, and long-term growth objectives.

At Forth Worth Studios, he leads brand discovery, client strategy, market research, and positioning, helping transform websites from simple digital assets into meaningful business tools.

AM
Aish MadaanCo-Founder & Creative Director

Aish is the creative mind behind Forth Worth Studios.

His journey began while building his own fashion brand, where he realized that many growing brands were forced to choose between generic templates and expensive agency solutions. Unsatisfied with both, he started designing and developing websites that felt as distinctive as the brands they represented.

Beyond Forth Worth Studios, Aish has founded multiple ventures, including Turiya, a clothing brand, and Resentening, a creative design studio. Through these experiences, he developed a deep understanding of branding, visual storytelling, digital design, and modern consumer culture.

At Forth Worth Studios, he leads design, development, creative direction, and digital experience strategy—transforming ideas into immersive online experiences that customers remember.

03

The Founder Dynamic.

Strategy Meets Creativity.

Great digital experiences require more than beautiful visuals. They require an understanding of both business and design.

At Forth Worth Studios, every project benefits from two perspectives: strategic thinking and creative execution.

While Utsav focuses on brand positioning, audience understanding, and business objectives, Aish focuses on design, interaction, storytelling, and user experience.

Together, this allows us to create websites that not only look exceptional, but also serve a clear purpose for the businesses behind them.
