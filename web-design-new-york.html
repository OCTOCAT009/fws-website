Web Design For US Brands.

A Delhi studio working with US businesses. The time difference is real and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

Being straight about where we are

No New York office. We are in Delhi, and working with us from the US means working asynchronously. That suits some clients extremely well and frustrates others, and it is better to know which you are before anyone signs anything.

The time difference, honestly

New York runs nine and a half to ten and a half hours behind Delhi. The overlap is our evening against your morning — enough for a scheduled call, not enough for casual back-and-forth.

So we work the way that actually suits that: written updates instead of standups, decisions batched rather than drip-fed, and scoping done properly upfront because a misunderstanding costs a full day rather than five minutes. If you want someone reachable on Slack all afternoon, hire locally — we will tell you that on the call.

The flip side is real: you finish your day, we work, and you wake up to progress.

What you get out of it

  • Delhi pricing on US work. A custom multi-page build starts at ₹15,000 — roughly $425. US studios quoting the same brief are typically an order of magnitude higher.
  • No account layer. Two people. You talk to the builders.
  • You own the code. No licence, no lock-in, no retainer required.

The honest caveats

We invoice in USD. We are not a US entity, so if you need a domestic vendor for compliance or procurement reasons, we are not it. And the asynchronous rhythm is genuinely the biggest variable in whether this works — it is the thing to weigh, not the price.
