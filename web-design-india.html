Built for how India actually browses

Most of your traffic will arrive on a mid-range Android phone over a patchy mobile connection. That single fact should shape every decision, and it is the one most agency sites ignore while showing you a desktop mockup.

It is why we self-host fonts, compress every image, and build the static page before adding motion. A beautiful site that takes five seconds to become useful has already lost.

The things that matter here and nowhere else

  • Cash on delivery — still a large share of Indian ecommerce, and almost always implemented badly. An enforced convenience fee is the difference between COD being viable and being a loss.
  • WhatsApp — where Indian customers actually want to talk to you, not a contact form
  • UPI and Indian gateways — set up properly, with the ~2% transaction reality stated upfront
  • DPDP Act 2023 — real compliance obligations around consent and opt-out, not a cookie banner copied off another site

Pricing, published

Indian web pricing ranges from ₹3,000 to ₹3,00,000 for the same brief, and almost none of that spread is explained to the buyer. We publish every number on the pricing page instead — tiers, add-ons, retainers and what each includes. If a quote you receive elsewhere will not explain what the extra buys, that is worth asking about.
