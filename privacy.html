Privacy Policy.

Short version: we collect what you send us through the contact form so we can reply to you. We don't sell it, and we don't run advertising trackers.

Last updated — 12 August 2026

Who We Are.

Forth Worth Studios is a web design and development studio based in Delhi, India, working with clients worldwide. For anything in this policy, contact us at hello@forthworthstudios.space.

What We Collect.

Information you give us. When you submit the form on our contact page we receive your name, email address, what you're building, your budget range, your message, and a note of which page or referrer you arrived from. That's it — we don't ask for anything else, and nothing on this site asks for payment details.

Information collected automatically. If website analytics are enabled, we collect aggregate usage data — pages viewed, approximate region, device type and referrer. This is used to understand which pages are useful. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Third Parties We Use.

This site is deliberately light on third-party services. The ones it does use:

  • Formspree — receives and forwards contact form submissions to our inbox. See their privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this site. Google receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • ipapi.co — on the services page only, used once to guess your country so prices can display in your local currency. We send no personal data to it and store nothing from it beyond your currency preference.
  • Website analytics — if enabled, an aggregate analytics provider as described above.

Cookies & Local Storage.

We don't set advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores a few small values in your own browser to remember your preferences:

  • fw_cur — the currency you chose on the services page.
  • fw_loader_seen — so the intro animation only plays once per visit.
  • fw_startbar_x — so a bar you dismissed stays dismissed.

These never leave your device and are cleared when you clear your browser data. If analytics are enabled, that provider may set its own cookie.

How Long We Keep It.

Enquiries are kept in our email and project records for as long as needed to respond and, where a project goes ahead, for the life of the working relationship plus any period required for tax and accounting. If an enquiry doesn't turn into a project, you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your Rights.

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@forthworthstudios.space and we'll action it. Depending on where you live — including under the EU/UK GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act — you may also have the right to object to processing, request a copy of your data in a portable format, or complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes To This Policy.

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
